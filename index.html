Why Stillkind

A softer kind of atmosphere.

We believe a home does not need to be perfect to feel peaceful. Sometimes it only needs a familiar scent, a warmer light, and permission to slow down.

Stillkind candles are designed in the Philippines with considered fragrance stories and a clean, understated look that belongs naturally in your space.

A better burn

Candle care, kept simple.

  1. 01Trim the wick

    Keep it around 5 mm before every burn for a steadier flame.

  2. 02Let the wax reach the edge

    On the first burn, allow a full melt pool to help prevent tunneling.

  3. 03Burn within sight

    Place on a heat-safe surface, away from drafts, children, and pets.
